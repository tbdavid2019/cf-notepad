# 888wiki

**An AI-ready wiki, writing workspace, and publishing platform built on Cloudflare Workers.**

Write in Markdown or blocks, organize ideas into a personal knowledge base, and publish secure, shareable pages from infrastructure you control.

[![Deploy to Cloudflare](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/button)](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/?url=https://github.com/tbdavid2019/888wiki)

[Live site](https://wiki.david888.com) · [API](https://wiki.david888.com/api) · [Agent Skill](https://wiki.david888.com/.well-known/agent-skills/david888-wiki-publisher/SKILL.md)

## What you can do

- **Write your way:** Use a live Markdown editor, BlockNote, canvas, and whiteboard editors with themes, offline drafts, and fast previews.
- **Create with AI:** Format, rewrite, and translate drafts; turn audio into timestamped transcripts; work with MCP-compatible AI agents.
- **Publish with control:** Share public or password-protected notes, manage revision history, add paragraph discussions, and present content as slides.
- **Keep your data on your account:** Deploy to Cloudflare Workers with KV, D1, and R2. The setup provisions resources and initializes the D1 tables.
